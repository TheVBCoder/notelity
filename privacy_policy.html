Privacy Policy

Notelity  ·  Last updated: June 2026

1. Overview

Notelity is a local-first notes application. Your notes, media, and personal data are stored on your device and never transmitted to any server operated by us. We do not collect, sell, or share your personal information.

2. Data We Store (On Your Device Only)

The following data is created and stored exclusively on your device:

  • Notes — text content, titles, and formatting
  • Media files — photos, videos, and audio attachments added to notes
  • Tasks and reminders — task lists, due dates, and alarm schedules
  • Spreadsheets — any spreadsheet data created within the app
  • App settings — preferences such as theme, layout, and security settings

None of this data leaves your device unless you explicitly initiate a backup (see Section 4).

3. Encryption

All note content and media files are encrypted on your device using AES-256-GCM. Notes you choose to lock are additionally protected by a password-derived key using Argon2id. Your password is never stored in plaintext — the cryptographic key is stored in the Android Keystore, which is hardware-backed on supported devices.

4. Backups

Notelity does not perform automatic cloud backups. If you use the in-app backup feature, the backup file is encrypted and saved to a location you choose (such as local storage or a folder you select). We have no access to your backup files.

Google's automatic Android backup is disabled for this app. Your encrypted files and database are excluded from any system-level backup.

5. Permissions

Permission Reason
Camera Capture photos and videos directly into notes
Read / Write Storage (Android 9 and below) Access media files on older devices
Internet Required for Google Play purchase verification (billing only) and local media streaming between internal app components
Notifications Display task reminders and alarms
Exact Alarm Schedule reminders at precise times
System Alert Window Display the widget note-entry overlay

The Internet permission is used exclusively for Google Play Billing and an internal localhost-only HTTP server that streams encrypted media within the app. No data is transmitted to external servers.

6. Third-Party Services

The only third-party service this app communicates with is Google Play Billing, used to verify your one-time Pro purchase. This communication is handled entirely by the Google Play Store app on your device. We do not receive or store any payment information. Google's privacy policy applies to this interaction: https://policies.google.com/privacy

We do not use any analytics, crash reporting, or advertising services.

7. Children's Privacy

Notelity does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13. The app does not collect personal information from anyone.

8. Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
